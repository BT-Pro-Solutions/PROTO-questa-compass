Compass Resource Provider Participation Agreement

The Compass Resource Directory is an online tool designed to help students, families, and student supporters find the resources they need to pursue further education and training after high school.

The goals of the Compass Directory are to:

  • Create a coordinated regional system to leverage local student resources more effectively, ultimately increasing postsecondary enrollment and completion
  • Help students and their supporters find trustworthy resources
  • Create multiple “front doors” where students, families, and supporters can get help
  • Foster referrals and collaboration among resource providers
  • Improve availability and impact of local resources

Resource Providers are organizations, primarily serving northeast Indiana, that provide one or more resources that aid students in achieving postsecondary education.

Commitments of Resource Providers:

  • Provide resources that are free and/or have subsidized access for students with financial need OR
  • Provide education and training programs as a non-profit, accredited organization or employer-provided program
  • Communicate services offered and any limitations or restrictions with transparency
  • Respond in a timely way to student and network member inquiries
  • Avoid unlawful discrimination in provision of resources and services
  • Designate a point person to provide periodic updates and ensure organization’s resources are current in the Compass Directory
  • Provide an easily accessible link to the full Compass Directory on their website (Alternative: If your organization cannot add a link on your website, please commit to promote the Compass directory in other ways – e.g. through handouts, presentations, word-of-mouth, etc.)

Resource Categories

  1. Learning Help – resources that help learners develop the academic skills they need to enroll and succeed in further education after high school.
  2. Careers – resources that help learners identify potential careers that fit them, explore career paths and related majors or training programs, develop the skills needed for professional success, and/or find opportunities to observe or gain experience in various careers.
  3. Education Readiness – resources that help learners explore college and training options, prepare for success in further education, and/or leverage college credit opportunities
  4. Funding – resources that help learners understand and plan for education expenses, complete the FAFSA, and/or provide funding assistance for education (scholarships and other aid)
  5. Personal Help – resources that help learners address personal needs (such as childcare, transportation, wellness, or social connections) to help them succeed in further education
  6. Education & Training – colleges, universities, training programs, and other providers of education and training beyond high school

Benefits & Tools for Resource Providers:

  • Promotion of your organization and resources
  • Referrals from network members & navigators
  • Learn More Earn More Marketing Assets
  • Learn More Earn More Discussion Guides
  • Compass Icon for marketing use
  • Compass network convenings and best practice sharing

The Compass Icon is a visual identifier designed to:

  • Help students, families, and student supporters quickly identify resource providers and the type of resources offered at an organization
  • Visually demonstrate the breadth and depth of local resources available to help students achieve further education
  • Identify a starting point for students, families, and student advocates to get information and referrals to other resources

Use of the Compass Icon: Resource Providers can use the appropriate Compass Icon to designate what learners will find at their organizations. Questa will provide a brand guide and high-resolution images for resource providers to use on websites, digital and print marketing, as desired.
